Filtering and the mental model

The job Product listing (PLP) filters aren't performing.
The industry Bridal gowns. The company is a global leader in the wedding-gowns industry. Its sites serve both consumer and wholesaler audiences — brides and bridal boutiques.
The business goal Increase engagement with the PLP.
My role Two functional hats: Product manager and UX designer. My team is only just learning the ropes of product ownership and management, and we have no dedicated designer, so delegating is not an option.

Engagement with the product filters is low. Visitors land on a PLP, may scroll partway down, then navigate to a different gallery. The prominent “Filter & Sort ” unused.

Why is that important? Engagement is a proxy for likelihood of purchase. Essense does not sell online. The objective is for the potential customer — the bride — to attach to a specific dress or the brand, then seek out a bridal boutique that carries the brand. The more the bride actively engages with the product selection, as opposed to simply browsing, the more likely that attachment is.
